What qualifies

  1. It is funny. Not relatable, not interesting. Funny.
  2. It is about selling something — a listing, a price, a haggle, a flip, a marketplace message, a thing somebody is trying to move.
  3. Or it is a found object — the beautiful junk somebody photographed in the wild.
  4. It stands on its own. No thread, no context, no inside joke required.
  5. Reposting it would not embarrass us tomorrow.
More criteria to come. This list is the whole spec for now, and it is the same list SOC SOCIALIZER BOT is handed when it goes looking.

That's Funny!

That's Funny!
Drag that button up to your bookmarks bar. Then, on any page anywhere that qualifies, click it.
It grabs what it can and drops it into this queue as NEW.
On X it reads the post itself. Everywhere else it takes the page's own title and description, or whatever you highlighted before clicking.
If dragging does not work
  1. Right-click your bookmarks bar and choose Add page (Chrome) or Add bookmark (Firefox, Safari).
  2. Name it That's Funny!
  3. Paste the whole block below into the URL field and save.
