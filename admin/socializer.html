Where it goes

Every one of these is a cut and paste method. Nothing on this page talks to any platform: it holds the words, you press Post.

The bookmarklet

Drag That's Funny! to your bookmarks bar, then click it on any page worth keeping. On X it reads the post itself; everywhere else it takes the page's own title and description, or whatever you highlighted first.

If dragging does not work

  1. Right-click the bookmarks bar and choose Add page (Chrome) or Add bookmark (Firefox, Safari).
  2. Name it That's Funny!
  3. Paste the block below into the URL field and save.
